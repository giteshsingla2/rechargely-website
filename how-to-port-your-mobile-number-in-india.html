How to Port Your Mobile Number in India: Step-by-Step MNP Guide

Unhappy with your network or prices? Switch operators and keep your number in a few days.

How-to guide · 5 min read

In this guide
  1. Before you port
  2. Step-by-step
  3. How long it takes
  4. After the switch
  5. FAQs

Before you port

  • Check coverage. Borrow a friend's SIM from the new operator and test it at home and work.
  • Check eligibility. Your number must generally have been active with the current operator for at least 90 days.
  • Clear dues. Postpaid bills must be paid before your request is accepted.
  • Use up your balance. Prepaid balance and remaining plan benefits do not move to the new operator.
  • Keep ID ready. The new operator will complete KYC, usually with Aadhaar.

Step-by-step

  1. 1
    Get your UPCFrom the number you want to port, SMS PORT <10-digit number> to 1900. You'll receive a Unique Porting Code by SMS.
  2. 2
    Request a new SIMVisit the new operator's store or book a SIM through its app or website. Share your UPC.
  3. 3
    Complete KYCVerify your identity. Choose prepaid or postpaid.
  4. 4
    Wait for the switchYou'll get an SMS with the switch time. Your old SIM stops working shortly before the new one activates.
  5. 5
    Insert and rechargePut in the new SIM, restart your phone and pick a plan.
UPC validity: the code expires after a limited number of days (longer in a few regions). If it expires, simply request a new one.

How long it takes

Porting within the same telecom circle usually completes in a few working days; porting to a different circle can take a little longer. You'll have a short window without service during the switch — usually at night.

After the switch

  • Recharge right away — compare the new operator's plans.
  • Check that bank OTPs and UPI still work on the number.
  • Update your operator app and remove the old one.
  • WhatsApp and other apps keep working, since your number hasn't changed.

FAQs

Can my request be rejected?

Yes — usually for unpaid dues, a wrong UPC, a number active for less than the minimum period, or mismatched KYC details.

Can I cancel a port request?

You can generally cancel by SMS to 1900 within a short window after requesting. Check the instructions in your UPC message.

Will my number stop working while porting?

Only briefly during the actual switch. Until then, your current SIM keeps working normally.

Porting rules are set by TRAI and can change. Check the latest rules on TRAI's website or with the new operator.
