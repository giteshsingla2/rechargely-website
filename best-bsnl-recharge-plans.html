BSNL plan guide · 4 min read

BSNL is India's state-owned operator, with wide reach in many rural and remote areas. It's often the cheapest way to keep a number active, but its plans work a little differently from private operators. Below we explain what to watch for and who BSNL suits, then list every plan with its true cost per day.

Why people choose BSNL

BSNL is popular as a low-cost secondary SIM and for long-validity plans that keep a number active for months at a low price. Its plans are often cheaper per day than private operators, though coverage and data speeds vary a lot by area.

  • Secondary SIM: long-validity plans keep the number active for the lowest cost.
  • Budget primary SIM: 30-day unlimited plans with daily data.
  • Check coverage first: ask neighbours or check signal on a friend's BSNL SIM before switching.

Read the fine print on long-validity plans

Some BSNL long-validity plans give full benefits (data, calls, SMS) only for the first part of the validity, and after that keep the SIM active with incoming calls only. This is great for a backup number but not for daily use. The plan cards below mark these with an asterisk.

BSNL 4G and 5G

BSNL is rolling out 4G and 5G in phases. If 4G is live in your area, you may need a 4G SIM — older SIMs can be swapped at a BSNL customer service centre.

How to read the plan cards

The big number is the price; the figure beside it is the cost per day. "Most popular" marks the plan most people pick, and "Best value" marks the lowest cost per day. An asterisk means some benefits last for only part of the validity — read the note under that plan. Tap Select to save your details and finish the recharge in the Rechargely app.

How to check your BSNL balance

Use the BSNL Selfcare app, or dial BSNL's balance USSD code (commonly *123#). If it stops working, check BSNL's official website.

BSNL recharge FAQs

What is the cheapest way to keep a BSNL SIM active?

A long-validity plan is usually the cheapest per day. Check the "Long validity" tab above.

Does BSNL work in all areas?

Coverage varies widely. It is often strong in rural areas and government buildings, but data speeds can lag behind private operators in cities.
