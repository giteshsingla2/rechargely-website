Home Broadband vs Mobile Data: Which Is Cheaper for Your Family?

If everyone at home is burning through 2–3 GB a day, a fibre connection might cost less than all those recharges combined.

Do the maths for your home

Add up what every person spends on recharges in a month. Then note how much of that is spent mainly for data — the higher-tier plans and data packs. That's the money broadband could replace.

Example: 4 people each on a ₹449/month 3 GB/day plan spend about ₹1,800 a month. With home Wi-Fi, each could drop to a cheaper 1.5 GB/day plan and a basic fibre plan could cover the rest — often for less in total, with faster speed at home.

When broadband makes sense

  • Two or more people at home regularly run out of daily data.
  • Someone works or studies from home on a laptop.
  • You want to stream on a smart TV.
  • Mobile signal indoors is weak.

If you live alone, are rarely home, or move often, a good mobile plan is usually enough. See how much data you really need.

Bundles and add-ons

Operators increasingly bundle fibre broadband with mobile connections, streaming apps or landline calling. A bundle is worth it when it replaces things you already pay for. Check:

  • Whether the mobile connections included are prepaid or postpaid.
  • Whether streaming apps are full plans or mobile-only versions.
  • Installation fees, router deposits and lock-in periods.
  • The price after any introductory offer ends.

Choosing a broadband plan

  1. Check availability — fibre must reach your building.
  2. Pick speed by people, not by headline. For most homes, a modest fibre plan handles several phones streaming at once.
  3. Prefer longer billing cycles — quarterly or annual payments often include a discount.
  4. Ask neighbours which provider has the fewest outages in your area.

FAQs

Can I cancel mobile data entirely with broadband?

You still need a plan to keep your number active and for use outside the home. A lower-tier plan is usually enough.

Is fibre better than a 5G home router?

Fibre is typically more consistent. 5G home routers are a good option where fibre isn't available, but speed depends on signal.
