Airtel plan guide · 5 min read

Airtel is one of India's two largest operators, with 5G available in many cities and a prepaid range that runs from basic validity packs to plans bundled with streaming apps. Below we explain how the plans differ and who each one suits, then list every plan with its true cost per day.

Which Airtel plan should you choose?

Airtel offers monthly unlimited plans on both 28-day and calendar-month (30-day) validity, long-validity plans for 84 and 365 days, OTT bundles, and data packs. The right choice depends on how much data you use each day and whether you value bundled streaming.

  • Budget pick: a 1–1.5 GB/day monthly plan covers messaging, browsing and light video.
  • Best for 5G phones: 2 GB/day plans typically include unlimited 5G in coverage areas.
  • Set and forget: 84-day and annual plans have the lowest cost per day.

28-day vs 30-day Airtel plans

A 28-day plan means 13 recharges a year; a 30-day or calendar-month plan means 12. If two plans look similar in price, the 30-day one is usually better value — compare the per-day figure on each card.

Are Airtel OTT plans worth it?

OTT plans bundle a streaming subscription with your recharge. They save money only if you would pay for that subscription anyway. Add the price of a normal plan to the price of the subscription on its own; if the OTT plan is cheaper, it is a good deal.

How to read the plan cards

The big number is the price; the figure beside it is the cost per day. "Most popular" marks the plan most people pick, and "Best value" marks the lowest cost per day for everyday use. Airtel postpaid and broadband bundles

If several people at home use Airtel, a family postpaid plan or a fibre broadband bundle with mobile connections can cost less than separate prepaid recharges. Compare the combined monthly cost before switching — our prepaid vs postpaid guide shows how.

How to check your Airtel balance and data

Use the Airtel Thanks app, or dial Airtel's balance USSD code (commonly *121#) from your Airtel SIM. If a code stops working, check Airtel's official website for the latest one.

Airtel recharge FAQs

Does my unused daily data carry forward?

Usually not — daily data resets at midnight. Some plans include data rollover; check the plan benefits in the app.

Can I use a data pack without a base plan?

No. Data packs need an active unlimited or validity plan.

Which Airtel plan is best for hotspot use?

Pick a 2 GB/day or higher plan, or a plan with unlimited 5G if your phone and area support it.
