About Rechargely: Who We Are and How to Contact Us

Rechargely helps you find the right prepaid plan for your SIM. We list plans from Jio, Airtel, Vi and BSNL side by side, show the real cost per day, and explain what each plan includes in plain language.

How recharges work

This website is for comparing plans and saving your details. Payment and the recharge itself happen in the Rechargely app. We show the final price in the app before you pay.

Independence
