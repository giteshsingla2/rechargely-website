Jio plan guide · 5 min read

Jio has the largest subscriber base in India, and one of the biggest prepaid line-ups too — daily-data plans, long-validity plans, data add-ons and OTT bundles. Below we explain how the plans are structured and who each type suits, then list every plan with its true cost per day so you can compare like for like.

Which Jio plan should you choose?

Jio's prepaid plans fall into three groups: monthly unlimited plans (28 days), long-validity plans (84 days and a full year), and data-only packs that add extra data to an active plan. Most people get the best value from the 2 GB/day monthly plan or the 84-day plan if they recharge regularly.

  • Light users (social media, messaging, some YouTube): a 1.5 GB/day plan is usually enough.
  • Regular streamers: 2 GB/day plans give headroom for daily video.
  • Heavy users and hotspot users: 3 GB/day plans, or pair a smaller plan with a data pack on busy days.

Jio 5G: do you need a special plan?

Unlimited 5G data is typically offered on Jio plans of 2 GB/day and above, as long as you have a 5G phone and are in a 5G coverage area. On lower plans, your daily data limit applies even on 5G. Check the plan's benefits in the app to confirm 5G eligibility before you pay.

Monthly vs 84-day vs annual Jio plans

Longer plans cost more upfront but less per day. Compare the "per day" figure on each plan card below — it is the fairest way to compare a 28-day plan with an 84-day or 365-day plan. If you are sure you will keep your Jio number for the year, the annual plan is usually the cheapest option.

How to read the plan cards

The big number is the price; the figure beside it is the cost per day. "Most popular" marks the plan most people pick, and "Best value" marks the lowest cost per day for everyday use. How to check your Jio balance and data

You can check remaining data and validity in the MyJio app, or by dialling the balance USSD code from your Jio SIM. Codes can change, so if one does not work, check Jio's official website.

Jio recharge FAQs

Can I recharge before my current Jio plan ends?

Yes. The new plan is usually added to the queue and starts after the current plan expires.

Do data packs extend validity?

No. Data packs add data only and need an active base plan. They end when your base plan ends.

Are OTT plans worth it?

Only if you would otherwise pay for that streaming service. Compare the OTT plan's price with a normal plan plus a separate subscription.
