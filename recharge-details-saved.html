While you're here: 5 ways to spend less on recharges

  1. Compare price per day, not price. Long-validity plans are almost always cheaper per day.
  2. Match the plan to your real data use. Check last month's usage in your phone settings.
  3. Use card or UPI cashback offers. Many cards give extra rewards on telecom bills.
  4. Skip OTT bundles you won't watch. They only save money if you'd pay for the service anyway.
  5. Consider a family postpaid plan. Sharing data across 3–4 connections can beat separate prepaid plans.

A household of four that follows just the first three tips can easily save ₹1,500–₹3,000 a year on recharges. See our full data needs guide to pick the right tier next time.

Earn rewards when you pay in the app

You're about to pay for this recharge — so pay in the way that earns you the most. Some cashback credit cards give a higher reward rate on telecom and utility bills; others give nothing on these payments. UPI apps also run frequent recharge offers. On a long-validity or annual plan, the right card can return ₹100–₹200 in a single payment. Just make sure you pay the full card bill on time, because credit card interest wipes out any reward. Read how to get cashback on recharges.

Protect the phone you just recharged

Screen repairs on modern smartphones can cost thousands of rupees. Mobile phone insurance or an extended warranty covers accidental and liquid damage, and some policies cover theft too. When comparing plans, look at the yearly premium, the amount you pay per claim, how many claims are allowed, and whether repairs use genuine parts. If your phone is new, check whether your brand or credit card offers screen protection at a discount.

Is home broadband cheaper for your family?

If everyone at home regularly runs out of daily data, a fibre broadband plan may cost less than buying bigger recharges for every phone. With Wi-Fi at home, each person can usually drop to a cheaper mobile plan. Many operators also bundle broadband with family postpaid connections and streaming apps. Compare your total monthly spend before switching — our broadband vs mobile data guide walks through the maths.

Unhappy with your network?

If calls drop or data is slow where you live, you can switch operators and keep your number through Mobile Number Portability. SMS PORT <your number> to 1900 to get your porting code. It's worth doing after your current plan ends so you don't lose unused days. Full steps in our porting guide.

Stay safe when recharging

Real recharge services never ask for your UPI PIN or an OTP to receive money, and never promise free recharges in exchange for sharing links. If a message offers a "free recharge" for forwarding it to friends, it's a scam — delete it. If you've lost money to a scam, call the cyber crime helpline 1930 straight away. Can I change the plan?

Yes. Pick a different plan on this website or choose another plan inside the app before paying.

When does the new plan start?
