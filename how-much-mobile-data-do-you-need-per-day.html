How Much Mobile Data Do You Need per Day? 1.5 GB vs 2 GB vs 3 GB

Stop guessing between 1.5 GB, 2 GB and 3 GB plans. Here's what each activity really uses.

Data guide · 6 min read

Check your real usage first

Your phone already knows how much data you use. On Android, open Settings → Network & internet → Data usage (the exact path varies by brand). On iPhone, go to Settings → Mobile Service and scroll to see usage by app. Note the total for the last 30 days and divide by 30 — that's your average daily use.

Look at the apps at the top of the list. Usually one or two video apps account for most of your data. That tells you where savings are possible.

Which daily plan fits you

1 GB/day — light users

Messaging, calls, maps and some browsing. Works if you're on Wi-Fi at home and work.

1.5 GB/day — most people

Social media, an hour or so of short videos, music on the commute. This is the most popular tier for a reason.

2 GB/day — regular streamers

Daily YouTube or OTT in SD, frequent video calls. On many operators this tier also unlocks unlimited 5G data on eligible phones.

3 GB/day and above — heavy users

HD streaming, hotspot for a laptop, or no home Wi-Fi at all. If you're here every day, compare a home broadband plan — it may be cheaper.

Rule of thumb: pick the tier just above your average daily use. Buying a data pack 2–3 times a month costs more than moving up one tier.

Ways to use less data

  • Set video apps to "Data saver" or a lower default quality on mobile data.
  • Turn off autoplay for videos in social apps.
  • Allow app updates and photo backups on Wi-Fi only.
  • Download playlists and shows on Wi-Fi to watch offline.
  • Use your operator's night data benefits for big downloads, if your plan includes them.

FAQs

What happens when my daily data runs out?

Speed usually drops to a low limit until midnight. You can buy a data pack to restore full speed for the rest of the plan.

Does 5G use more data?

5G itself doesn't, but faster speeds make apps load higher-quality video, so usage often rises. Set video quality manually if you're on a limited plan.

How much data does hotspot use?

A laptop uses far more than a phone — software updates and cloud sync alone can use several GB. Turn off automatic updates while tethering.
