Vi plan guide · 4 min read

Vi (Vodafone Idea) tends to compete on extras rather than headline price — night-time data, weekend rollover and bonus data offers. Those extras can make a smaller plan go much further, so it pays to understand them first. Below we explain what they mean and who each plan suits, then list every plan with its true cost per day.

Which Vi plan should you choose?

Vi plans often include extra data benefits on top of the daily limit — such as night-time data or weekend rollover of unused data. These extras can make a lower daily-data plan go further, so read the benefits on each card, not just the headline data.

  • Night owls: plans with unlimited night data suit people who download or stream late.
  • Uneven users: weekend rollover lets you save weekday data for the weekend.
  • Lowest cost per day: the 84-day and annual plans.

What do "Binge All Night" and "Weekend Rollover" mean?

Night data benefits give unlimited data during set late-night hours without using your daily limit. Weekend rollover carries unused weekday data forward to Saturday and Sunday. Exact timings and limits vary by plan — check the plan details in the app.

Is Vi good value compared with Jio and Airtel?

Headline prices are often similar across operators. The difference is in the extras and in network quality where you live. If Vi coverage is strong in your area, the bundled data benefits can make it the better deal. See the comparison table on the home page.

How to read the plan cards

The big number is the price; the figure beside it is the cost per day. "Most popular" marks the plan most people pick, and "Best value" marks the lowest cost per day for everyday use. Extras like night data are listed with a tick under each plan. Tap Select to save your details and finish the recharge in the Rechargely app.

How to check your Vi balance and data

Use the Vi app, or dial Vi's balance USSD code (commonly *199#) from your Vi SIM. If a code stops working, check Vi's official website.

Vi recharge FAQs

Does Vi rollover data expire?

Rolled-over data is generally usable until the end of the weekend or plan validity, depending on the plan's rules.

Can I port from Vi to another operator?

Yes. SMS PORT followed by your number to 1900 to get your porting code, then request a SIM from the new operator.
