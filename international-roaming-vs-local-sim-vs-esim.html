International Roaming Pack vs Local SIM vs eSIM: What's Cheapest Abroad?

Three ways to stay connected abroad — and how to keep your Indian number working for bank OTPs.

Travel guide · 6 min read

Your three options

1. International roaming pack from your Indian operator

Activate a pack for your destination before you fly. Your Indian SIM works abroad with a fixed bundle of data and calls. It's the most convenient option and keeps your number reachable, but usually the most expensive per GB.

2. Local SIM at your destination

Buy a prepaid SIM after landing. Usually the cheapest for data on longer trips, but you need a dual-SIM phone (or to swap SIMs) to keep your Indian number active, and some countries require ID registration.

3. Travel eSIM

Buy a data-only eSIM online and install it before you travel. No shop visit, and your Indian SIM stays in the phone. Your phone must support eSIM and be unlocked.

Keeping OTPs working

Banks and UPI send OTPs by SMS to your Indian number. To receive them abroad:

  • Keep your Indian SIM active with a valid plan — recharge before you leave.
  • Check that incoming SMS works on roaming; on many plans incoming SMS is free or very cheap.
  • If you use a local SIM or eSIM for data, keep the Indian SIM in the second slot with data roaming turned off.

Tips to avoid bill shock

  • Turn off data roaming on your Indian SIM unless you've bought a pack.
  • Download offline maps and entertainment before you fly.
  • Use Wi-Fi calling where your operator supports it.
  • On postpaid, set a spending alert or credit limit.

FAQs

Do I need to activate roaming before leaving India?

Yes, usually. Some operators require international roaming to be switched on in the app before it works abroad.

Will WhatsApp work on a local SIM?

Yes. WhatsApp stays linked to your Indian number even when you use another SIM for data.
